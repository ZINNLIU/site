Game Theater — Robot Theater Series 博弈剧场——机器人剧场系列影像
Artwork
▼
刘紫茵、高源 LIU Ziyin (Zinn), GAO Yuan (Alex)

收藏于深圳湾文化广场

Collected by Shenzhen Bay Cultural Plaza

「博弈剧场」将机器人围捕实验中的异构多智能体的"演员-评论家"协同框架，引入一个极简、抽象的拟真实验剧场中，将人形、轮式、四足机器人及无人机等多构型机器人，设定为追捕者、躲避者、通讯员、交涉者、观察员、代理人、工人等角色，通过交涉、协作、追捕、规避、博弈等策略与机器人形成不断生长的剧场与动态关系，仿佛一个缩微的人机社会结构：持续迭代、策略演化。作品以多视角影像，揭示了一种由人、技术与组织构成的复杂交织状态，而观众亦作为人类节点与观察者视角参与剧中。

Game Theater introduces the heterogeneous multi-agent 'actor-critic' collaborative framework from robotic research experiment, into a minimal and abstract simulated experimental theater. Within this arena, robots of diverse configurations including humanoid, wheeled, quadruped, and aerial drones are assigned roles such as catcher, runner, communicator, negotiator, observer, obstacle and agent. Through strategies of negotiation, collaboration, chase, evasion, and game dynamics, humans and robots co-construct a evolving theatrical and performative field. The dynamic system resembles a miniaturized human-robot social structure, with constant iteration and strategic evolution. Through diverse perspectives, this work reveals a complex entanglement of humans and technological systems. Audience also participates as a human node and observer as it unfolds...

About

刘紫茵(ZINN)，科技艺术策展人，艺术项目策划管理，现任南方科技大学 博物馆创新研究院副院长；博物馆智能机器人实验室(筹)负责人；曾任深圳市人工智能与机器人研究院(AIRS)科学+艺术联合实验室主创、第十届深港城市\建筑双城双年展(深圳)UABB执行策展人、MIT媒体实验室驻留策展人与人机交互驻留会主席。

ZINN (LIU Ziyin) is a curator of art and technology and an art project planner/manager. She is currently Deputy Director of the Museum Innovation Institute (MII) at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) and Head of the Museum Intelligent Robotics Laboratory (in preparation). She formerly served as Founding Member of the SciArt Co-Lab at the Shenzhen Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society (AIRS), Executive Curator of the 10th SZ-HK Bi-City Biennale of Urbanism\Architecture (Shenzhen) (UABB), Curator of the MIT Media Lab Residency, and General Chair of the HCI Residency Symposium.

曾作为AIRS研究院科技艺术展览"问题的问题"、"问题演绎"执行策展人，参与多项城市公共展馆策展与城市文化活动策划，研究实践方向探索科学艺术交叉领域的叙事与策展，跨学科共创与国际协作机制。

She was Executive Curator of AIRS's art&tech exhibitions The Question of the Question and The Deduction of the Question, and has participated in the curation of multiple public museums and urban cultural programs. Her research and practice explore narrative and curation at the intersection of science and art, as well as interdisciplinary co-creation and international collaboration mechanisms.
